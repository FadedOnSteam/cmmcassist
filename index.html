Is your company ready for CMMC Level 1?

Level 1 applies to companies that handle Federal Contract Information (FCI): non-public information you receive or create under a government contract, like pricing, schedules, or order details. It has 15 requirements that must all be met to achieve level 1 CMMC readiness.

How to answer: answer Yes only if it’s true today for every computer, account, and space that holds contract information. If you’re not sure, answer No.

About

What is CMMC, and which level do I need?

The Cybersecurity Maturity Model Certification (CMMC) is the Department of Defense’s program for checking that contractors protect sensitive information. Which level applies depends on what information your company handles.

Level 1

You handle FCI only: non-public contract information like pricing, schedules, and order details.

15 requirements · yearly self-assessment

Level 2

You handle CUI: information like technical drawings, specs, or export-controlled data that’s marked CUI.

110 requirements · NIST SP 800-171

Level 3

You work on the most sensitive programs, and the contract says Level 3 is required.

Level 2 plus NIST SP 800-172

About this tool

CMMC Assist turns the official requirements into plain-language yes-or-no questions so a small business owner, not just an IT expert, can see where they stand. It’s a student project, built from the public CMMC Assessment Guides and NIST SP 800-171.

This is a self-check, not an official CMMC assessment, and it isn’t affiliated with the Department of Defense. Your answers stay in your browser and aren’t sent anywhere.
